Privacy Policy — Troop Equipment

Last updated: 2025

1. What data we collect

  • Names — entered when submitting tickets or purchase requests
  • Phone numbers — optionally entered on ticket forms
  • Photos — optionally uploaded to purchase requests
  • Group affiliation — the group a user selects when logging in

2. How data is stored

All data is stored locally on the device in browser localStorage. If the troop connects a Google Sheet, ticket and note data is sent to that sheet over HTTPS via Google Apps Script. We do not operate any servers or cloud storage.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties. Data may be transmitted to Google Sheets only if the administrator has configured this integration.

4. Children's privacy

This app is used by a scout troop that may include minors. No data is shared externally. All data remains on the device or within the troop's own Google account.

5. Data deletion

Admins can delete individual tickets, purchase requests, and feedback at any time. All data can be wiped using "Reset all data" in admin settings.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed using SHA-256. Inputs are sanitised to prevent scripting attacks. The app enforces a Content Security Policy and uses no third-party tracking.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact your troop administrator.
